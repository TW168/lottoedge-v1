Generate Picks

Configure strategy weights and generate optimised number combinations.

Settings
  • Game — Which lottery to generate tickets for.
  • Number of picks — How many different ticket combinations to generate.
  • Include historical eras — Texas Lotto had a different format 2003–2006 (Pick 5 + Bonus instead of Pick 6). Off by default because mixing formats skews results.
  • Diversity Level — Low = picks share many numbers (safer, concentrated). High = picks spread across the full number pool (wider coverage).
Module Weights — what each one scores
  • Frequency — How often a number appeared in the last 30 draws.
  • Positional — Whether a number tends to land in a specific sorted position.
  • Cluster/Pairs — Numbers that frequently appear together with other strong candidates.
  • Due Score — Numbers that haven't been drawn for longer than their usual gap (overdue).
  • Momentum — Numbers whose recent frequency is rising vs. their long-term average.
  • Short-term Heat — Numbers drawn in just the last 10 draws.
  • Group Balance — Rewards picks that spread across number decades (1–9, 10–19 …). Most winners span 4–5 groups.
  • ML/LSTM & Ensemble ML — Machine learning models (Random Forest + Gradient Boosting) trained on historical draw features.
  • Monte Carlo — Simulates 50,000 draws weighted by history; numbers that appear most often get a higher score.
  • Coverage — Favours numbers that increase unique pair coverage across your full set of tickets.
Every number is scored 0–100 by blending all 11 signals using your weights. The generator then picks combinations that pass statistical filters: correct odd/even balance, high/low balance, sum within the 70% historical range, and good group distribution.
